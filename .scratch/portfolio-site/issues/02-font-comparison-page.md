# 02 — Font comparison page

**What to build:** A hidden page where Shainy can compare 3–4 type pairings (display + body, optionally a mono accent such as IBM Plex Mono), each rendered with real ShainyDev headline and body text, in both dark and light themes, alongside candidate peach/coral accent shades. After Shainy picks, the chosen fonts and accent become site-wide design tokens. All fonts must be free for commercial use (e.g. Google Fonts, Fontshare).

**Blocked by:** 01 — Walking skeleton, deployed

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The comparison page is reachable by URL but not linked from navigation
- [ ] Shows 3–4 distinct pairings, each at hero size and body size, in dark and light
- [ ] Shows 2–3 candidate peach/coral accent shades on dark and light backgrounds
- [ ] **Checkpoint:** Shainy has picked a pairing and an accent shade
- [ ] The chosen fonts and accent are applied site-wide as tokens; fonts load efficiently
